# Copy, SEO & Pricing (Phase 4)

Channel-specific. The biggest mistake is reusing Shopify-style copy on Etsy.

## Etsy — search-first copy

Etsy buyers search **themes/occasions**, not your business name. Lead with the theme; the
brand/story is flavor.

**Title (≤140 chars):**
`[Theme] Shirt, [Variation] Tee, [Recipient] Gift, Vintage [Category] T-Shirt, [Decor/Occasion]`
- e.g. `Vintage Whiskey Shirt, Antique Liquor Ad Tee, Bourbon Lover Gift, 1900s Distillery T-Shirt, Bar Cart Decor, Mens Whiskey Gift`

**Tags:** exactly **13**, each **≤20 chars**, long-tail phrases, minimal overlap with the title
words (tags should *add* coverage, not repeat). e.g. `whiskey shirt`, `bourbon gift`,
`bar cart decor`, `speakeasy shirt`, `1900s shirt` …

**Description:** first **~160 chars** carry Google SEO — front-load keywords, then the story, then
specs (blank/material/fit + a size table). No source-archive talk.

**Required compliance:** designate the production partner (Printify) and mark "I designed it /
another company made it." Confirm the POD disclosure is on.

## Shopify — your own store

You control layout and can lead with the business/brand story. Still write real SEO titles + meta
descriptions per product (the phishing-y "your store is missing meta descriptions" spam is
annoying but the underlying point is real). Collections + clear navigation matter because there's
no built-in search traffic — you're bringing it.

## Pricing (value, not cost-plus-panic)

- Anchor to **perceived value + channel norms**, not just cost. Vintage-ad tees priced ~$26.99;
  keep a healthy margin (~$10–13 over blank+print) after fees.
- Etsy fees to net against: 6.5% txn + ~3%+$0.25 processing + $0.20 listing.
- Use **price ranges** across variants/sizes; a small "sale from" anchor can lift conversion.
- Bundle/tiered pricing for multi-buys where it fits (e.g., stickers).
- Don't race to the bottom — POD's floor is high; compete on design/niche, not price.

## Copy guardrails

- **Never** mention public domain, DigitalNC, or "old newspaper archive" in customer copy — it
  cheapens the product and invites IP questions.
- Don't imply endorsement by any brand depicted.
- Keep an **AT-LINT-style copy check** (fail-closed) if automating: reject any listing whose copy
  contains banned phrases before it can publish.
